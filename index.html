DAYZERO — A Scholar's Journal

Your ambitions deserve a system.

DAYZERO is a vintage-inspired academic productivity platform designed to bring studying, planning, revision, and self-improvement into one focused space.

Built for students who want to take ownership of their learning, DAYZERO combines an intuitive study planner, academic progress tracking, SAT preparation, a learning library, a weakness ledger, and a dedicated Notes Studio in a single workspace.

From organising daily tasks and tracking exam deadlines to identifying knowledge gaps and practising challenging concepts, every feature is designed to make studying more intentional and progress more measurable.

Wrapped in a timeless, scholarly aesthetic inspired by traditional journals and university libraries, DAYZERO offers a calmer alternative to cluttered productivity tools.

Plan with purpose. Study with intention. Make every day count.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
